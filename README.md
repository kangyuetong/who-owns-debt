# Who owns the world's debt?

An interactive financial research dashboard exploring external debt, debt-service pressures, and reported creditor relationships using World Bank data.

### [Explore the interactive dashboard](https://kangyuetong.github.io/who-owns-debt/)

## Explore the data

Compare 2024 external debt stocks and annual debt-service flows across Indonesia, India, Brazil, Mexico, and South Africa. Explore an interactive world map, debt-profile similarity network, country comparisons, and a hypothetical refinancing sensitivity tool.

Examine reported 2024 public and publicly guaranteed (PPG) external debt creditor shares for Senegal and Samoa, including individual named creditors and broader creditor groups.

## How to interpret the findings

Debt-service-to-stock ratios compare annual payments with outstanding debt; they are not interest rates or formal measures of debt distress. The debt-profile network represents similarities between economies, not actual lending relationships. Creditor breakdowns refer to PPG external debt rather than all external debt, and aggregated categories do not identify ultimate beneficial owners.

## Sources

Data and country profiles: [World Bank International Debt Statistics](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/debt-statistics/ids) and [International Debt Report 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/debt-statistics/idr). Values are rounded for display and may be revised by the source.
