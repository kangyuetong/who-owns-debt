Reading the evidence

Total external debt includes public and private liabilities, while the creditor breakdown below refers only to public and publicly guaranteed (PPG) external debt. Similarity-network connections are calculated comparisons, not lending relationships. Bondholders and “other” categories do not reveal ultimate beneficial owners. Figures should be reconciled against the linked World Bank series and country reports before citation or publication.

DATA INTEGRITY · Figures are sourced from World Bank International Debt Statistics, displayed in US$ billions rounded to two decimals. The dashboard covers five selected reporting economies, not every country or all worldwide debt. Named creditor exposures are displayed only for Senegal and Samoa, sourced to published World Bank country profiles. Other creditor-level relationships are not inferred. World Bank data are subject to revisions. World Bank IDS methodology.
